ShopTill

A silent till for Even G2 glasses. Say the price, the customer pays in Telegram, and your glasses say PAID only when the TON chain confirms it. Screenshots don't count.

Who it's for

Crypto-native sellers, face to face

P2P USDT traders, booths at TON events, Telegram sellers who meet buyers in person. Their buyers already have a TON wallet.

The two traps

A "payment sent" screenshot that never arrives, and a look-alike token that reads as USDT. Checking an explorer means looking away from the person in front of you.

How it works

Pair in Telegram

Send the code on the glasses to @ShopTillBot, name the shop, connect the wallet that gets paid, print the counter QR.

Say the price

Tap the glasses and say "twelve dollars fifty". It shows what it heard and waits for your tap.

Customer pays

They scan the QR, the Mini App opens with the amount filled in, and they approve in their own wallet. Money goes wallet to wallet.

The chain decides

PAID only for a finalized transfer of the real token, with this sale's reference, the right amount, on time.

Limits, said plainly

  • Runs in the Even Hub simulator; not yet tested on physical G2 hardware.
  • Till mode: the seller opens ShopTill per sale. G2 can't alert while the app is closed, so late payments go to the bot instead.
  • The buyer must already use TON. ShopTill doesn't onboard new crypto users.
  • You trust this server to read the chain. If it can't, the glasses say Offline or "check delayed, do not hand over". Every receipt links to tonviewer, and the code is open so you can run your own.
  • ShopTill never holds money or keys.
