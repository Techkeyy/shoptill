03How it works

One minute to set up. All inside Telegram.

Pair the glasses

Send the code on the glasses to @ShopTillBot. Name your shop.

Connect a wallet

TON Connect reads only the public address that gets paid. Print the counter QR.

Say the price

Tap the glasses: "twelve dollars fifty". It shows what it heard and waits for your tap.

They pay, chain decides

Scan, Mini App, approve in their own wallet. Money goes wallet to wallet. Glasses say PAID.

04Behind the glasses

Five checks, in order. Every time.

  1. It succeededFailed and bounced transfers never count.
  2. It's the real tokenThe token's contract must be on the allowlist. Look-alikes show FAKE.
  3. It carries this sale's referenceEach customer gets their own reference, so payments can't be mixed up.
  4. The amount is rightToo little shows SHORT, too much shows EXTRA.
  5. It's on timeLate money is flagged as LATE, never silently counted.
Never holds money or keys

The customer pays the seller directly. ShopTill only reads the chain.

Never says PAID early

A payment that is seen but not final shows "confirming", not PAID.

Silence never means paid

If the chain can't be read for 15 seconds, the glasses say "check delayed, do not hand over yet".

06Said plainly

What it isn't, yet.

Simulator, not hardware. Built and tested in the Even Hub simulator. Not yet run on physical G2 glasses.

Till mode. The seller opens ShopTill per sale. G2 can't alert while the app is closed, so late payments go to the bot.

Buyers already use TON. ShopTill doesn't onboard new crypto users.

You trust this server to read the chain. It says so when it can't, links every receipt to tonviewer, and the code is open.
